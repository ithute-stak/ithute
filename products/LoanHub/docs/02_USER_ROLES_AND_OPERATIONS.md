# User Roles and Operations

## Platform owner / SuperAdmin
Govern tenants, plans, platform accounting, reports, incidents, audit and technical operations.

## Company owner
Highest tenant authority for settings, branches, staff, products, lending, billing, HR, accounting and reports.

## Company administrator
Runs day-to-day company administration and cross-department coordination.

## Branch manager
Controls branch staff, lending work, collections, performance and branch reports.

## Loan officer
Reviews marketplace requests, unlocks authorised details and creates offers.

## Finance officer
Controls disbursements, repayments, reconciliation, accounting and financial evidence.

## Collections officer
Manages due and overdue accounts and records collection follow-up.

## Compliance officer
Reviews access, audit events, regulatory controls and evidence.

## Auditor
Performs independent, read-oriented review of operations and financial evidence.

## Customer support
Assists users through authorised records, notifications, chat and safe escalation.

## HR manager
Maintains employee records, reporting lines and protected workforce information.

## Performance manager
Creates goals, reviews progress and evaluates individual/branch/company performance.

## Risk manager
Reviews credit, operational, payment and platform risks.

## IT support
Investigates incidents and availability without bypassing business controls.

## Borrower
Creates requests, compares offers, manages personal loans, payments, chat and files.

## General operating rules

1. Always confirm the active company and branch before changing records.
2. Use friendly business references rather than raw UUIDs in support communication.
3. Do not mark a payment successful without verified provider or approved manual evidence.
4. Store sensitive HR, compliance and finance evidence with confidential visibility.
5. Ordinary users should report the request reference shown by a safe error message; technical diagnostics are restricted to platform owners.
