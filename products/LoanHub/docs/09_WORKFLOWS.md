# Business and Technical Workflows

The diagram files are available as PNG, SVG and editable Graphviz `.dot` source.

1. `01-system-context` - runtime services and external actors.
2. `02-auth-tenant-flow` - authentication, role routing and tenant resolution.
3. `03-marketplace-flow` - borrower request, lender access, offers and acceptance.
4. `04-loan-payment-flow` - disbursement, repayment, allocation and accounting.
5. `05-notification-error-flow` - business notifications versus technical incidents.
6. `06-chat-file-security-flow` - message/file upload, encryption and authorised download.
7. `07-midnight-reconciliation-flow` - 00:00 Africa/Maseru processing.
8. `08-role-impersonation-flow` - audited platform-owner role switching.
9. `09-deployment-pipeline` - GitHub validation and Hostinger deployment.
10. `10-upgrade-change-map` - programmer decision flow for future modifications.
