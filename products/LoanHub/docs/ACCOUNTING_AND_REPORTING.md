# Accounting and Reporting

## Accounting foundation

LoanHub includes a double-entry accounting foundation for platform and company scopes.

### Core records

- Accounting accounts
- Draft and posted journal entries
- Debit and credit journal lines
- Transaction references
- Branch and company scope
- Posting user and posting time

### Available statements

- Trial balance
- Profit and loss
- Balance sheet

### Automatic transaction posting

Successful supported payment events can create balanced journal entries. Exact account selection must be reviewed against the organisation's approved chart of accounts and business rules.

### Controls

- Journal lines must balance before creation.
- Draft entries must be posted before appearing in final statements.
- Tenant context determines company books.
- Platform administration can operate platform books.
- Accounting events are included in transparency and audit records.

## Operational reporting

Reports can be generated in PDF or CSV.

### Frequencies

- Daily: prior day
- Weekly: prior completed week
- Monthly: prior completed month
- Annual: prior completed year

### Scopes

- Branch
- Company
- Platform

### Measurements

Depending on scope and available data, reports include:

- Companies and branches
- Active staff and employee profiles
- Loan requests engaged
- Offers created and accepted
- Active, completed and overdue loans
- Principal portfolio, outstanding balance and amount paid
- Successful and failed payment transactions
- Request-to-offer and portfolio-overdue rates
- Performance reviews and goals
- Average score and goal-completion rate
- Accounting revenue, expenses, net profit, assets, liabilities and equity

## Distribution status

The report centre stores generated documents and schedule recipients. Email sending is a future integration. Users should download and distribute reports using approved channels until email delivery is implemented.
