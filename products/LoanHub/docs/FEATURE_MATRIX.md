|---|---|---|---|---|
| Company approval and suspension | Full control | View own status | View relevant status | Not applicable |
| Branches and staff | Platform visibility | Create and manage | Branch-scoped according to role | Not applicable |
| Loan marketplace | Oversight | View and respond | Lending roles respond | Broadcast request and select offer |

| File centre | Platform files | Company/confidential files | Access by visibility and branch | Own/shared conversation files |

| Performance | Platform/company/branch | Company-wide | Branch or individual scope | Not applicable |
| Accounting | Platform books | Company books | Finance-authorised roles | Not applicable |
| Reports | Platform/company/branch | Company/branch | Authorised report roles | Personal information through operational pages |
| Audit activity | Full platform | Company-wide | Role and branch scope | Own notifications/actions |
| System errors | Full management | Safe tenant notification | IT/risk/compliance visibility | Safe user-facing errors only |

## Role summary

- **SuperAdmin:** controls the multi-tenant platform, companies, plans, platform finance, reports, errors and audit transparency.
- **Company owner:** highest tenant authority; controls settings, branches, staff, products, lending, billing, HR, finance and reports.
- **Company administrator:** operational tenant administration comparable to owner, subject to configured policy.
- **Branch manager:** manages branch operations, branch staff, lending workflow, collections and branch performance.
- **Loan officer:** evaluates requests, prepares offers and supports loan origination.
- **Finance officer:** manages disbursements, payments, accounting and financial reports.
- **Collections officer:** follows overdue loans and repayment activity.
- **Compliance officer:** reviews audit history, controls and regulatory exceptions.
- **Auditor:** read-oriented access to activity, performance and financial evidence.
- **Customer support:** assists borrowers and company users through permitted records and chat.
- **HR manager:** manages employee profiles and authorised workforce data.
- **Performance manager:** manages goals, reviews and performance reporting.
- **Risk manager:** reviews risk, exceptions, activity and reports.
- **IT support:** monitors system incidents and assists users without unrestricted financial authority.
- **Borrower:** creates requests, compares offers, manages loans, repayments, chat and personal files.
