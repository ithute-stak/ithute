**Secure business calls, client contacts, recordings and call transfers.**

## Full description

LoanHub is the employee business-phone companion for organisations using the LoanHub platform.

Authorised employees sign in with their existing company account to access the clients and calling tools permitted by their organisation. Customers do not need LoanHub installed — calls are routed through the organisation's configured business telephony service to ordinary telephone numbers.

With LoanHub, authorised employees can:

- find permitted LoanHub clients and relevant loan references;
- place controlled business calls to ordinary telephone numbers;
- optionally save authorised LoanHub client contacts to the Android phone book;
- mute and hold an active business call;
- transfer a live call to another configured LoanHub employee;
- record call outcomes and notes;
- view company call/recording policy information; and
- keep business calling associated with the correct company, employee, client and loan record.

Reliable two-way recording and public telephone routing use the organisation's configured SIP/PBX/media provider. Available calling, recording, inbound routing and transfer features therefore depend on the organisation's LoanHub and telecommunications configuration.

LoanHub Mobile is designed for authorised workplace users. It is not a consumer loan application and does not provide a public mobile loan-application service.
