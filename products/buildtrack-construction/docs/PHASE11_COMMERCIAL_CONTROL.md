# Phase 11 — Cost, Commercial, Client and Claims Control

Phase 11 turns BuildTrack's project-delivery records into controlled commercial information in Maloti (M). It extends Phase 6 project budgets, Phase 8 purchase-order commitments and Phase 9 subcontract commitments; it does not replace their original approval and audit trails.

## Delivered controls

- one controlled client-contract register per project, with retained contract evidence and maker/checker approval;
- client variations with value and time-extension evidence, controlled approval and current-contract-value calculation;
- immutable actual, accrual and adjustment cost entries by project, site, cost centre, type and cost code; corrections create an auditable negative reversal rather than editing history;
- approved client valuations, retention deductions and controlled client-invoice drafts;
- recorded client receipt evidence with over-receipt prevention; BuildTrack records proof only and never sends invoices or moves money;
- extension-of-time, disruption, payment, variation and other client claims with notice/response deadlines and controlled submission;
- monthly project cash-flow forecasts, linked project dashboards, budget/exposure/revenue/receivable/margin calculations, alerts, CSV export and commercial audit history;
- commercial manager, quantity surveyor and project commercial officer roles, scoped permissions, approval workflows and company policy defaults.

## Boundary

Commercial figures are company records and evidence, not accounting entries, tax calculations, bank instructions or legal advice. Users must attach and verify controlled contractual, valuation, invoice, receipt and claim evidence. A Phase 11 approval authorises a BuildTrack lifecycle state; it does not send a document to a client, collect cash, agree a claim or alter external accounting software.
