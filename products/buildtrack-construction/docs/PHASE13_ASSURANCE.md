# Phase 13 — Document, HSE and Quality Assurance

Phase 13 adds controlled project-document revisions and one evidence-led assurance register for RFIs, site instructions, permits to work, toolbox talks, HSE inspections, quality non-conformances and corrective actions.

Records remain project/branch/site-scoped and may reference existing controlled documents. Critical records need evidence before review. The creator submits the record, while a separately authorised user performs the independent close/return review. The module records the control state; it does not certify statutory compliance, issue legal instructions or replace professional HSE/quality judgement.
