# Phase 16 — Professional Rollout

Phase 16 adds an evidence-led, branch-by-branch production rollout gate. It records operational readiness; it does not claim that a backup, recovery, migration, security assessment, UAT test or training session happened unless staff attach and verify the corresponding company evidence.

## Each rollout wave includes

- automatic `RLW` reference, branch, owner and planned/actual go-live dates;
- required backup/restore point and recovery-test evidence;
- data-migration reconciliation, security-review/remediation and UAT sign-off records;
- user-training/support readiness and go-live communications controls;
- an optional-control register for branch-specific checks;
- a strict completeness gate: all required controls must be complete before review, and the default company policy requires a controlled document for each;
- independent readiness review: the creator cannot approve their own wave;
- launch confirmation only after the independent approval; and
- branch-scoped audit history and dashboard indicators for outstanding/overdue controls and rollout state.

## Operational boundary

The rollout register does not run backups, alter source data, execute a recovery, scan security, send communications or deploy production infrastructure. It is a controlled record of work performed and validated by authorised Nthane Brothers staff.
