# Phase 17 — Project Closeout & Defects Liability

Phase 17 gives Nthane Brothers one controlled route from practical completion to final project closure. It preserves the Phase 1 organisational backbone, Phase 11 commercial evidence, Phase 13 controlled documents and Phase 2 scoped access rather than creating parallel registers.

## Delivered controls

- one `PCO` closeout record per project, with practical-completion and defects-liability dates;
- a required closeout checklist for practical completion, as-builts, O&M manuals, warranties, client handover, final account, retention evidence, demobilisation and archive handover;
- controlled document references for every required evidence item, with recorded waiver reasons where a requirement is formally waived;
- final-account and retention-release amounts recorded as evidence only; BuildTrack does not create a payment instruction;
- a `CDF` defects-liability register with category, priority, due date, evidence and a controlled close-out document;
- high and critical unresolved defects block closeout approval by the default company policy;
- two-stage branch/HQ maker-checker approval using the shared Phase 1 approval engine and company no-self-approval policy;
- final project closure only after the approved defects-liability period ends and every defect is closed;
- closeout dashboard, controlled CSV export, branch/site scope enforcement and immutable audit history; and
- a browser workspace integrated with the shared dialog-form and filtered/paginated table system.

Practical-completion controls are frozen once the independent approval passes. Retention release and archive controls remain controlled during the approved defects-liability period and must be completed or formally waived before final project closure.

## Operational boundary

BuildTrack records supplied practical-completion certificates, as-builts, manuals, warranties, client acceptance, final-account and retention evidence. It does not invent completion certificates, release retention, send a payment instruction, certify legal compliance, or close a defect without authorised evidence.
