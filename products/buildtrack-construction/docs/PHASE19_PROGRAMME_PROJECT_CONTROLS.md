# Phase 19 — Programme & Project Controls

Phase 19 gives Nthane Brothers a controlled planning workspace without creating a second project, document, approval or claims system.

## Delivered controls

- versioned `PBL` programme baselines scoped to existing projects, branches and sites;
- draft activity networks with WBS codes, dates, weights, owners, milestone links and predecessor validation;
- exact 100% programme weighting, dependency-cycle prevention and calculated critical-path/variance indicators;
- Branch → Head Office maker/checker baseline approval, no-self-approval enforcement and automatic supersession of the previous approved baseline;
- cumulative progress/forecast history which cannot move backwards or change the approved baseline;
- a controlled rolling six-week lookahead, with work constraints, owners and status;
- `PDL` delay evidence register, notice-date watch, controlled raise/close workflow, dashboard alerts, CSV export and audit; and
- the `/planning` browser workspace integrated with the shared dialog-form and filtered/paginated data-table system.

## Operational boundary

The module works from real programme and event evidence. Critical-path flags, progress variance and forecast dates are operational planning indicators—not contractual findings. BuildTrack does not send notices, decide responsibility, calculate extension-of-time entitlement or replace the professional contract administrator.
