# Phase 22 — Integrated Compliance & Policy Control

Phase 22 provides project-scoped policy versions, supplied source-referenced compliance obligations, recurring review dates, evidence-led review records, maker/checker approval, operational alerts, CSV export and audit history.

It does not interpret legal rules, certify statutory compliance, send notices, create operational/commercial/payroll transactions, or replace Phase 13 assurance. All documents must already be active controlled BuildTrack documents. Policy versions freeze after approval; approval of a later version supersedes the earlier approved version. Attention and non-compliant reviews require a recorded corrective action.

Initialise it at **Compliance Control** (`/compliance`). The migration revision is `0023_phase22_compliance`.
