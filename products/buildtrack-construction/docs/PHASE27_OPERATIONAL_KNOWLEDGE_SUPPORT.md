# Phase 27 — Operational Knowledge, SOP & Support Centre

## Purpose

Phase 27 turns independently closed support work and released changes into a controlled, searchable operational knowledge base. It gives Nthane Brothers one branch/site-aware place for practical procedures, system guidance and troubleshooting notes without treating an informal note as an approved policy or legal statement.

## Delivered controls

- Phase 26 support tickets are now available in the browser Support Centre, including branch/site scope, priority, controlled resolution evidence and independent closure verification.
- Controlled knowledge articles receive a company-unique `KNO` number and retain branch/site ownership, audience, category, summary, practical guidance, evidence document, review due date and audit trail.
- A knowledge article may cite only a **closed** support ticket or **released** change from the same branch/site scope. This preserves learning without exposing unrelated operational records.
- The lifecycle is `draft → submitted → published → retired`. Submission requires an active controlled document and review date.
- Publication is maker/checker controlled. The author cannot publish their own article where the existing company no-self-approval setting disables it.
- Published articles can be acknowledged by authenticated staff; one acknowledgement per article/user is retained with actor and timestamp.
- Dashboard counts show published guidance, items awaiting independent publication, articles due for review in the next 30 days and acknowledgement activity.
- The Support Centre uses the shared polished workspace, dialogs for compact entry, confirmations for controlled actions, and global search/filter/pagination for every register table.

## Boundaries

Phase 27 records supplied operational guidance and evidence only. It does not create a legal/company policy (Phase 22 owns that), run HR training records (Phase 15 owns that), alter a production system, route a change (Phase 25 owns that), or close support work without Phase 26 independent verification.
