# Phase 28 — Environmental & Sustainability Control

Phase 28 adds project-scoped environmental plans, waste evidence and environmental inspections to BuildTrack.

- Plans use controlled documents, review dates and independent approval.
- Waste records retain destination, quantity, carrier reference and hazardous classification; hazardous waste requires evidence and independent verification.
- Environmental inspections move from open finding to evidence-backed remediation and independent verification.
- Records remain branch/site/project scoped and auditable. The dashboard shows plans, recorded waste, hazardous items awaiting review, and open/critical inspections.

This phase records supplied environmental evidence only. It does not state legal obligations, certify compliance, calculate carbon credentials, transport waste, or alter Phase 13 HSE/Quality evidence.
