# Phase 29 — Tools & Calibration Control

Phase 29 controls small tools separately from vehicles and heavy plant: branch/site ownership, custody to staff/projects, return condition, calibration certificates and independent verification. Measuring and safety tools cannot be issued when their required calibration is overdue or missing.

The module records supplied tool, custody and certificate evidence. It does not replace Phase 4 fleet/plant maintenance, invent a calibration certificate, buy equipment or certify legal compliance.
