# Phase 33 — Client Relationship & Account Management

Phase 33 adds branch/site-scoped client accounts, controlled contacts and a supplied feedback register. A feedback record keeps its date, category, 1–5 score, comment and remediation action. It can become verified only when a different authorised user confirms the recorded action plan.

This phase does not create a client portal, fabricate client satisfaction, alter contracts/tenders/projects, provide legal advice or replace Phase 21 project correspondence. Staff remain responsible for entering and validating actual relationship information.
