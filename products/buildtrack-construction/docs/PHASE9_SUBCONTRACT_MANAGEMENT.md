# Phase 9 — Subcontract Management Completion Contract

Phase 9 brings subcontract commercial control into BuildTrack without duplicating the Phase 1 organisational, approval, document or audit foundations.

## Completed operating scope

- subcontractor prequalification with compliance/insurance evidence, status, trade categories and performance score;
- project-owned subcontract packages and priced scope lines;
- invitations, complete bid capture, evaluation and maker/checker award;
- controlled contracts, variations, work certificates, retention and payment-proof recording;
- hard checks for inactive vendors, expired bids, incomplete scope pricing, bid competition, duplicate contracts, certificate over-certification and payment overrun;
- Subcontract Manager, Subcontract Officer and Site Commercial Officer roles;
- /subcontracts commercial workspace and /subcontracts/control governance workspace;
- dashboards, alert queues, contract/certificate exports and immutable audit events.

## Deliberate boundaries

Subcontract bids, award documents, measurements, tax amounts, variations and payment proof must be provided as real company evidence. BuildTrack records that evidence and enforces the workflow around it. It does not create bank payments or introduce tax calculations without verified legal requirements.
